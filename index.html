About

I'm Miles, an MS in Quantitative Finance student at Northeastern University graduating in December 2026, with a strong foundation in both finance and computer science. I currently work as a Software Engineer and Junior Designer at Adicot Engineering, where I build the firm's in-house HVAC load calculation and job-management software. Previously, I was a Junior Analyst at the 360 Huntington Fund, where I managed quantitative analysis for a ~$2M portfolio across 46 positions.

My work centers on uncovering market inefficiencies through systematic, data-driven approaches. Recently, I tested whether Polymarket's earnings-beat markets carry information that a firm's own beat history misses, built a framework that compares rate, spread, default, recovery, and prepayment sensitivities across seven credit instruments, and developed a Treasury ETF rotation strategy driven by Nelson-Siegel and Diebold-Li yield-curve signals. I combine rigorous quantitative analysis with practical implementation, building tested, reproducible pipelines that take a research question from raw data to a defensible conclusion.

With a computer science background from FAU and prior experience at Draper Laboratory developing analytical systems, I bring strong technical skills to financial problem-solving. I'm particularly interested in quantitative trading, derivatives markets, and building systematic strategies that exploit structural market inefficiencies.
